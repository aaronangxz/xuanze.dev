## What it is

<Draft>
Most wardrobes are full of clothes nobody remembers owning. Clozit starts by getting your wardrobe into your phone: take a photo, and the app works out what the piece is, its brand, material and category. From there you can style outfits from what you have, and list anything you don't wear to sell or swap with other people on the app.
</Draft>

## What's in it

- **Wardrobe**: photo-in, catalogue-out. Each item is analysed by AI in the background and confirmed by you before it's added.
- **Style**: build outfits from your own pieces, plus an outfit-of-the-day widget.
- **Marketplace**: list a piece to sell, swap or both, with offers, swap proposals and orders. Payments use PayNow.
- **Pop-up store**: a checkout flow for buying in person at the Clozit pop-up on 28–29 Nov 2026.

## How it's built

The iOS app is native SwiftUI. Behind it is a Go API server and a Go worker on Railway, sharing a PostgreSQL database. Slow work, like analysing a garment photo, goes on a Postgres-native job queue (River), so there's no Redis to run. Photos live in Cloudflare R2, sign-in is Firebase, and garment analysis calls the Claude API.

## Making sure it doesn't break

<Draft>
Every change ships with QA evidence. A screenshot harness can render any screen from fixed sample data, with no sign-in and no backend, and a script walks every route on the iOS Simulator, builds an HTML report and posts it to the pull request. It also guards against making a real, billed AI call by mistake during a screenshot run.
</Draft>
